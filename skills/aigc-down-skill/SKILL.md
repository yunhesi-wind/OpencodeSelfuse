# AIGC Down Skill

Reduce AIGC detection rate in Chinese academic writing through targeted rewriting.

## Why Text Gets Flagged
Chinese AI detection systems look for:
1. **Uniform sentence length** — AI tends to produce sentences of similar length
2. **Template-like transitions** — 首先...其次...最后...此外...总之
3. **Overly neat logical flow** — Real human academic writing is messier
4. **Repetitive sentence structures** — Same subject-verb-object pattern
5. **Low lexical diversity** — Same academic phrases recycled
6. **Perfect parallelism** — Three-part lists everywhere

## Rewriting Strategies

### Strategy 1: Sentence Length Variation
- Mix long (30+ 字) and short (< 10 字) sentences
- Break one long AI sentence into 2-3 sentences of varying length
- Occasionally use sentence fragments for emphasis

### Strategy 2: Transition Variation
Instead of always using 首先/其次/最后:
- Use semantic transitions: "相比之下", "值得注意的是", "更关键的是"
- Skip transitions sometimes: let content flow naturally
- Use questions as transitions: "那么, 为什么会这样?"

### Strategy 3: Lexical Diversity
Identify repeated phrases and replace:
- "结果表明" → "数据显示", "实验证实", "分析揭示", "我们发现"
- "重要的" → "关键的", "核心的", "本质的", "决定性的"
- "影响" → "作用", "效应", "调控", "干预"

### Strategy 4: Structural Variation
- Not every paragraph needs the same structure
- Some paragraphs: claim → evidence → explanation
- Others: question → exploration → finding
- Some: straight to the point, no preamble

### Strategy 5: Personal Voice
- Occasionally use "我们注意到" rather than purely impersonal
- Add qualifiers: "可能", "似乎", "在一定程度上"
- Include honest limitations: "需要更多数据来验证这一假设"

## Process
1. Read and identify patterns that trigger detection
2. Rewrite section by section, varying strategies
3. Preserve ALL data, citations, and factual claims exactly
4. Check: does the rewritten text still say the same thing?
5. Check: does it read like a real person wrote it?

## Warning
Do NOT use these strategies to falsify research or bypass plagiarism detection.
This skill is for legitimate academic writing improvement.
