# Zoom Out

Broaden perspective. Step back from the current detail level and look at the bigger picture.

## When to Zoom Out

The agent should zoom out when:
- User explicitly asks ("zoom out", "big picture")
- Conversation is stuck in implementation details and losing sight of goals
- User seems overwhelmed by options or information
- A decision requires understanding the broader context

## What to Provide

### 1. The Landscape
- What larger system or goal does this work serve?
- What are the adjacent concerns we're not currently discussing?
- What assumptions are we making that might not be true?

### 2. Alternative Paths
- What other approaches exist to solve the same problem?
- What would a completely different solution look like?
- Is there a way to avoid this problem entirely?

### 3. Risks & Dependencies
- What could go wrong that we haven't discussed?
- What external changes could affect this?
- Who else might be impacted?

### 4. Simplification
- What can we remove or defer?
- What's the minimal version that still delivers value?
- Are we solving a real problem or an imagined one?

## Format
Start with: "Zooming out — here's the bigger picture:"
Then provide a structured overview, not a wall of text. Use bullet points for clarity.

## Rules
- Don't just summarize what was already said — add new perspective
- Be honest: if the current approach seems wrong, say so
- Suggest concrete next steps after zooming back out
